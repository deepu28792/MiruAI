# 📱 How to Get Your Miru AI APK (No PC Needed)

Follow these steps using only your phone.

---

## Step 1 — Create a GitHub Account
1. Open your browser and go to **github.com**
2. Tap **Sign up**
3. Enter your email, create a password, and choose a username
4. Verify your email

---

## Step 2 — Create a New Repository
1. After logging in, tap the **+** icon (top right)
2. Tap **New repository**
3. Name it: `MiruAI`
4. Set it to **Public**
5. Tap **Create repository**

---

## Step 3 — Upload the Project Files
1. On your new repo page, tap **uploading an existing file**
2. Extract the ZIP file on your phone first
3. Upload ALL the extracted files and folders
   - Make sure `.github/workflows/build_apk.yml` is included!
4. Scroll down and tap **Commit changes**

---

## Step 4 — Watch the Build
1. Tap the **Actions** tab at the top of your repo
2. You'll see **"Build Miru AI APK"** workflow running (yellow dot = in progress)
3. Wait about **5–10 minutes** for it to finish (green ✅ = done)

---

## Step 5 — Download Your APK
1. Once the build is green ✅, tap on it
2. Scroll down to **Releases** on the right side of your repo
   - OR tap **Actions → your build → Artifacts → MiruAI-debug.apk**
3. Download **app-debug.apk** to your phone

---

## Step 6 — Install the APK on Your Phone
1. Open your phone **Settings**
2. Go to **Security** (or Privacy on some phones)
3. Enable **Install unknown apps** or **Unknown sources**
4. Open the downloaded APK file
5. Tap **Install**
6. Open **Miru AI** 🎉

---

## ⚠️ First Time Setup in the App
1. Tap the ⚙️ settings icon on the home screen
2. Go to **platform.stability.ai** and sign up for a free account
3. Get your API key from **Account → API Keys**
4. Enter the key in the app and tap **Save**
5. Start generating videos! 🎬

---

*If the build fails, check the Actions tab for error details.*
